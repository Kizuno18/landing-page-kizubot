::en
- added a preset in the actions tab to notify when someone watches your CAM for more than 10 minutes
- the **stucked alarm** message now also includes the exact coordinates of the stucked position and the waypoint index it was on
- reconnect is out of maintenance
- reconnect was moved from the **General > Geral** tab and is now part of the actions tab, with a "preset" available under "Quick actions"
- fixed an issue in the license decrement system that was not correctly deducting days
- fixed an issue from yesterday’s update where the "wrong character nick" error was still not showing in the panel login within VNC
- added a preset in the actions tab to notify when a friend from the VIP list comes online
- added a preset in the actions tab to notify when a friend from the VIP list goes offline
- alwaysPlayAlarms now has a "privateOnly" option to keep it active only for private messages, even when the bot is turned off
::
