::en
- the new premium packs come with 300h, 720h, or 2160h: hours are only used while your character is online in the game. log out and your balance pauses, unused hours don't expire, and renewing adds to the balance you already have
- we added cota to the dashboard: connect a paid, compatible AI subscription and you can use a machine with base resources without paying for another kizubot license. usage depends on the available quota, and you can recreate the machine when that quota renews
- new interactive map in the waypoint editor: build and adjust your route right on the game map, switch floors, search for pokémon and resources, add, drag, or delete points, undo changes, and repair sections of the path. we also fixed saving that claimed success without keeping your changes
- we rebuilt stun settings per pokémon: choose when to use it, including before revive, against special creatures, at combo start, or in an emergency. there are advanced rules, a copy-to-team option, and a guided dashboard tutorial
- you can now choose per extraordinary creature whether kizubot starts the combo right away or brings your pokémon closer and waits for the box. that wait also reacts to low character health or getting stuck. we added an option to return after the combo for a creature that appeared while the bot was busy, plus optional alarms for your list
- we improved actions that swap pokémon, heal, and return to the previous one, with priority, a sequence duration limit, and per-rule cooldowns of up to 24h. we fixed hotkeys that ignored combat conditions and revised the emergency healing preset
- we fixed support moves interfering with the combo, more summon-and-recall loops after reconnecting, fishing stuck waiting for unavailable moves, routes skipping stairs, and shinies missing from the catch queue. we also updated recognition of game items and corpses
- we fixed xp gains that dropped to zero or showed absurd values, and stats that stopped updating after some drops. recordings resume after death and a quick relog, more windows stop covering replays, and your graphics and audio choices survive relogging unless the dashboard sends new settings
- you can now create your account before buying a license and confirm your whatsapp number with a code. purchases accept cards and boleto, with access released after payment is confirmed. we simplified activation and fixed an issue that prevented using a license bought through your own account
- we improved alarms so new alerts don't get stuck behind old retries, fixed timestamps on delayed alerts, and added an alternate delivery path for critical anti-bot verification and gm alerts. we also fixed death and disconnection alerts being suppressed near NPCs
- we made more recreate fixes to stop failed attempts getting stuck, make machine recovery easier, and let you use a new license once the previous one has ended and the old machine has been released. we also fixed cases where your character was hunting but the dashboard still showed it joining the game
- kizubot turns 2 on october 11 and starts its third year: premium 720h is R$ 300 and 2160h is R$ 900 through november 5, inclusive, in Brasília time. from november 6 they become R$ 450 and R$ 1200; 300h stays at R$ 260. the site now has an english version, mobile improvements, and balloons you can pop

<p class="mt-4">base still runs on calendar days, for people who use the machine all day: for example, 3 characters in sequence, 8h each, totaling 24h a day, one character at a time. older calendar-day premium licenses don't automatically turn into hourly packs</p>

<p class="mt-4">the updated bot version is available on new machines and on your next recreation through the dashboard. machines already in use were not restarted to apply this update</p>
