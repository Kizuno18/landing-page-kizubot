- resolvido problema que a **IA** desativava a montaria mesmo não estando configurado
- corrigido problema que com player na tela adicionava a lista de catch pokemons mesmo com ksIgnoreCatch ativado
- removido LostLure e LostLoot do HUD já que não são mais usados pelo KizuBot
- corrigido problema que considerava "hora de lotear" mesmo sem nenhum corpo para pegar loot
- agora a **IA** considera um bush que tem player na frente ou coletando como já coletado até que saia da tela (evita problemas de ks de bush)
- agora o HUD ficou um pouco mais compacto pois estava usando espaço desnecessário
::

::en
- Fixed an issue where the **AI** would dismount even when the mount option was not enabled
- Fixed a bug where, with a player on screen, Pokémon were being added to the catch list even with ksIgnoreCatch enabled
- Removed LostLure and LostLoot from the HUD since they are no longer used by KizuBot
- Fixed a problem where it considered it was “time to loot” even when there were no bodies to loot
- The **AI** now considers a bush with a player in front of it or collecting as already collected until the player leaves the screen (prevents bush KS issues)
- The HUD is now slightly more compact as it was using unnecessary space
::
