::en
- The "Messages for Trade" option that was previously located in **General > Interactions** is now part of the **Actions** tab and includes a "preset" in "Quick actions".
- The "autoOpenPrivateChat" option was removed from **General > General** and is now part of the **Actions** tab with a "preset" in "Quick actions".
- Added a pre-configured "preset" for fishing in **Manage Profiles**.
- Improved the **Catch** tab function assureCanThrowBall.
- Various improvements made in the **Actions** tab for some options and conditions.
- It's now possible to properly scroll through multiple actions.
- Added a "preset" in the **Actions** tab to "use stun based on my Pokémon's HP%".
- Added a "preset" in the **Actions** tab to "detect player, safely log out, and log back in after 5 minutes".
- Fixed internal login issues in the control panel within VNC — now it will display the specific error when you enter something wrong instead of showing "no server response".
::
