- Added **Quick Action** in the **Actions** tab to automatically sell a predefined item on the market via hotkey

- Added **Quick Action** in the **Actions** tab to deposit all money into the vault via hotkey
- Added **Quick Action** in the **Actions** tab to withdraw all money from the vault via hotkey
