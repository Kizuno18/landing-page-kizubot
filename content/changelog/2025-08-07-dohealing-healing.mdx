- alwaysPlayAlarm saiu a aba engine e foi movido para o topo da aba alarms

::en
- taught the **AI** to detect 20 new shinies
- fixed the "Delete" hotkey that hides/shows the bot buttons
- improved the description of the **doHealing** option in the **HEALING** tab in the panel
- added a new option in the alarms tab to better control the LowSupplies alarm, allowing custom configuration
- character information on the dashboard is now updated every 3 seconds
- waypoint list is now updated every 3 seconds, so after a "record" you no longer need to press F5
- added a box in the alarms tab to ignore any alarm about a specific item based on its name
- added Anti-Spam for duplicate alarm messages with configurable delay
- added a box in the alarms tab to ignore any alarm about specific creatures based on their name
- AlarmEnabled was removed from the engine tab and moved to the top of the alarms tab
- alwaysPlayAlarm was removed from the engine tab and moved to the top of the alarms tab
- disableAlarmsNearNurseJoy was removed from the engine tab and moved to the top of the alarms tab
- disableAlarmsNearMark was removed from the engine tab and moved to the top of the alarms tab
