::en
- fixed issue in the "Logout when player on screen" action and in some other actions
- taught the **AI** to ignore NPC Agatha, since hideBrotherHood was not covering it
- for those who create waypoints manually, we added a new "mode" called wait
- fixed occasional issue where the Bot would go after loot from other players’ creatures even when configured to ignore
::
