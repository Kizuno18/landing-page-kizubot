::en
- The design of kizubot.com has been updated
- The checkout page design has been updated
- Pressing **F12** now opens a panel displaying all bot hotkeys
- The panel design has reverted to the old style, with improved text visibility
- Pressing **F11** now shows real-time bot statistics (reworked old HUD)
- Pressing **F10** now displays real-time data on how the bot's AI is thinking
- Fixed an issue where login and password were not being saved on the initial bot login after recreate and loading settings.otml
- Applied a fix for combos that require more than one revive, ensuring the Pokémon is repositioned based on your `orderInBestTile` settings
- The bot now **always** collects loot from extraordinary creatures, even if looting is disabled
- Fixed an issue where `Combat > lootingEnabled` being disabled caused freezing when extraordinary creatures were killed
- Fixed false positives generated by the **Anti-GM** system (it sometimes detected player Pokémon)
- Fixed cavebot and loot issues during hunts of **Banette**
- Fixed cavebot and loot issues during hunts of **Blaziken**
- Fixed cavebot and loot issues during hunts of **Shelgon**
- Fixed cavebot and loot issues during hunts of **Claydol**
- Fixed cavebot and loot issues during hunts of **Gardevoir**
- Fixed cavebot and loot issues during hunts of **Sableye**
- Fixed cavebot and loot issues during hunts of **Alakazam** in **Ruins Cave - Johto**
- Fixed cavebot and loot issues during hunts of **Metang**
- Fixed cavebot and loot issues during hunts of **Mawile**
- Now, if after 3 seconds the total number of monsters on screen is already within the box and you are still "waiting for lure" or luring, the bot will detect that it is better to combo, assuming no more Pokémon are coming
::
