::en
- Added option to send messages in trade (engine tab)
- Added 4 new alarms:
- Stone drop (now separate from rare drop)
- Alarm if someone invited you to a party
- Message in the party channel
- Message in the TV channel
- Fixed 2 bugs where the **AI** would get "stucked" and didn't know what to do
- Now the low supply alarm notifies the remaining quantity
- Several security improvements implemented 🤫
- Fixed a minor issue in the corpse drawing system where the **AI** often got confused
- The revives per hour HUD has been activated and will track revives per hour
- Added button and hotkey to stack money (only works on the market's Pokémon Center machine)
- Added `reviveOnAllMovesInCooldown` in Healing, which will use revive if all the cooldowns (both target and area) you configured in setup are in cooldown
- Added option to enable/disable killing extraordinary Pokémon such as: shiny/boss/mega
- Fixed a bug where the **AI** wasn't correctly calculating the `avoidLostMultiplier` in cavebot
- Added ultra secret master blaster option that you will only discover by digging
- Optimizations in AI's processing cost
::
