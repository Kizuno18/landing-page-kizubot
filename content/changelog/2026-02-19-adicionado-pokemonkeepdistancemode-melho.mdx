- corrigido problema na condição `pokemon_dead` da aba actions
- corrigido bug que mantinha `Pokemon Keep Distance` ativado mesmo que `Player Positioning During Combat -> Keep Configured Distance` estivesse desativado
- melhorado design e experiencia do usuario na aba setup-poke
- corrigido pequeno bug na nova função da aba Farming `stepInTallGrass`
- resolvido um bug que impedia de dar dex ou dar order corretamente
- adicionado pokemonKeepDistanceMode pra você poder escolher se quer que seu pokemon "fuja" dos inimigos sempre (bom pra farm de headbutt) ou apenas "fuja" até a hora de aguardar a box
- seeparamos Player Positioning During Combat de Pokemon Keep Distance
::

::en
- Fixed issue with the `pokemon_dead` condition in the Actions tab
- Fixed a bug that kept `Pokemon Keep Distance` enabled even when `Player Positioning During Combat -> Keep Configured Distance` was disabled
- Improved design and user experience in the Setup-Poke tab
- Fixed a minor bug in the new Farming tab function `stepInTallGrass`
- Resolved a bug that prevented giving Dex or issuing orders correctly
- Added `pokemonKeepDistanceMode`, allowing you to choose whether your Pokémon should always "flee" from enemies (useful for headbutt farming) or only "flee" until it needs to wait for the box
- Separated `Player Positioning During Combat` from `Pokemon Keep Distance`
::
