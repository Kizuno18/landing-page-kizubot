- Adicionado em "Quick Actions" na aba **Actions** um preset que monta no veículo após 1.1s sem Pokémon invocado, agilizando deslocamento fora de combate.
- adicionada action pra usar veiculo da profissão na aba **actions**
- corrigido bug na action de banir player da cam que bugava o alarme em loop
- corrigido pequeno problema que causava alguns delays nos avisos de licenças
- ajustados 17 presets de "auto use item" dos itens de 0.5 de drop pra funcionar so fora de pz
- corrigido problema no dashboard que a aba conexão não estava gerando QRCode para whatsapp
- adicionados talk_to_npc, npc_select_option e buy_npc_item como opções de action a aba **Actions**
- corrigido problema que eventualmente quando vc dava tp pra sair da hunt e n tinha coletado algum loot a **IA** não entendia
::

::en
- Added a quick action in **actions** tab that mounts the vehicle after 1.1s without a summoned Pokémon, speeding up travel outside combat.
- Added an action to use the profession vehicle in the **Actions** tab
- Fixed a bug in the action to ban a player from the camera that caused the alarm to loop
- Fixed a minor issue that caused some delays in license warnings
- Adjusted 17 "auto use item" presets for 0.5 drop items to work only outside of PZ
- Fixed an issue on the dashboard where the Connection tab was not generating a WhatsApp QR code
- Added talk_to_npc, npc_select_option and buy_npc_item as action options in the **Actions** tab
- Fixed an issue where, eventually, when you teleported to leave the hunt without having collected some loot, the **AI** would not understand
::
