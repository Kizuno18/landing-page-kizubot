::en
- Fixed an issue when throwing a ball at Kecleon automatically.
- Optimized AI structure to **reduce the number of mandatory** `!recreate` commands.
- The AI has now learned to "dash," resulting in a **noticeable speed increase** when using Cavebot.
- Minor fix in the **alarm system**.
- Several optimizations were made to **Cavebot**.
- Fixed a bug in the **Recorder** where, after sending waypoints to the website, the AI **still remembered the route** and mixed scripts.
- The AI **no longer considers** messages from the following NPCs as private messages:
- **Biker Punk**
- **Meowth Hunter**
- **Meowth Huntress**
- The AI **won't consider itself "stuck"** if solving a minigame.
- Improved **Cavebot for fly mechanics** (currently, it can only be used to travel between locations—not very useful yet).
- Added **an extra layer of protection** against bans and detection.
- **Relog** = **CTRL + G** and **Enter**
- **Restart** = **ALT + F**
- **Recreate** = Open chat with **+5548991594946**, type `!view` and `!recreate <machinename>` <This message was edited>
